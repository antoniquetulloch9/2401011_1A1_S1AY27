Using AI as a Tool, Not a Crutch

As a student, I think LLM's such as ChatGPT, Claude and Copilot can be helpful when they are used responsibly. They can explain difficult topics in simpler language, suggest examples and help students identify areas they need to study further.

In my Computer Network and Security programme, I could use an AI tool to help me understand networking concepts, Linux commands or cybersecurity terminology. For example, I could ask for a simpler explanation of a concept and then check it against my lecture notes and reliable sources.

However, depending on AI to complete every assignment can prevent students from developing their own problem-solving and writing skills. AI can also provide incorrect information, so its answers should not automatically be treated as facts.

I believe AI should support learning rather than replace it. I should attempt my work first, use AI when I need clarification, verify the information, and make sure I can explain the final work myself. I must also follow my lecturer's rules about acceptable AI use.
