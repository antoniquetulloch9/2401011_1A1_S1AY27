Introduction

In this section, I reflect on two chapters from George S. Clason's The Richest Man in Babylon: "7 Cures for a Lean Purse" and "5 Laws of Gold." My aim is to understand the lessons and consider how they relate to my own decisions and future.

Reflection: 7 Cures for a Lean Purse

What I understood: One of the main lessons I took from The Seven Cures for a Lean Purse is that saving money requires discipline. I believe saving is a financial habit that everyone should practise, regardless of how much money they earn. Instead of spending everything I receive, I should learn to put aside a portion of my money and manage my expenses wisely.

How it relates to my life: As a university student, learning to budget and save can help me become more financially responsible. I also understand that building wealth takes time, patience, and good decisions. My financial goal is not simply to be rich but to be wealthy, meaning that I want to build financial stability and have enough resources to support myself and my future goals.]

What I could change: My biggest takeaway is that financial success starts with the habits I develop today. Even small amounts saved consistently can help me build a stronger financial future.

Reflection: 5 Laws of Gold

What I understood: The Five Laws of Gold teach the importance of managing money wisely and making careful financial decisions. One lesson I find important is that having money is not enough; a person must also know how to protect it and use it properly. I believe that saving money is a form of financial discipline because it requires a person to think about the future instead of spending only for the present.

How it relates to my life: My goal is to become wealthy rather than simply appear rich. To me, being wealthy means having financial security, making wise decisions with money, and being prepared for unexpected expenses. I also believe that learning new skills and improving my knowledge can help me create better opportunities for myself.

My Biggest Takeaway

My biggest takeaway is that building wealth requires patience, knowledge, and discipline. I cannot expect to achieve my financial goals overnight, but I can work towards them by developing good habits and avoiding careless financial decisions.
